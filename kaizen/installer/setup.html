Welcome to Kaizen 5S

Kaizen tidies your PC with the Japanese 5S method. It finds files and programs you no longer use, shows each one with a thumbnail and a one-line AI explanation, and helps you sort, clean and keep things in order. The AI runs locally on your PC, and nothing is uploaded.
