# Images, video and audio. Image helpers keep the originals; ffmpeg is used for video/audio.

## Images

? convert {ext} to {ext2} | convert all {ext} files in {folder} to {ext2} | change {ext} images to {ext2} | turn {ext} into {ext2}
Get-ChildItem -Path "{{folder}}\*" -File -Include {{ext}} | Convert-Image -Format {{ext2|raw}}
% ext=images ; ext2=images
= Writes a {ext2} copy of every {ext} image in {folder}; the originals stay (Sensei helper).
& folder=.

? convert {file} to {ext} | save {file} as {ext} | change {file} to {ext}
Convert-Image -Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}" -Format {{ext|raw}}
% file=images ; ext=images
= Writes a {ext} copy of {file}; the original stays.
& folder=.

? resize {file} to {percent} | resize the image {file} to {percent} | resize the photo {file} to {percent} | make {file} {percent} smaller | scale {file} to {percent} | shrink {file} by {percent}
Resize-Image -Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}" -Percent {{percent}}
% file=images
= Writes a resized copy of {file} at {percent}% (name-resized.ext); the original stays.
& folder=.

? resize {file} to {n} pixels wide | resize {file} to width {n} | make {file} {n} px wide
Resize-Image -Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}" -Width {{n}}
% file=images
= Writes a copy of {file} that is {n} pixels wide; the original stays.
& folder=.

? resize all {ext} in {folder} to {percent} | shrink all {ext} in {folder} | make all {ext} smaller
Get-ChildItem -Path "{{folder}}\*" -File -Include {{ext}} | Resize-Image -Percent {{percent}}
= Writes a {percent}% copy of every {ext} image in {folder}; the originals stay.
& folder=. ; percent=50

? resize all {ext} in {folder} to {n} pixels wide | resize all {ext} to width {n}
Get-ChildItem -Path "{{folder}}\*" -File -Include {{ext}} | Resize-Image -Width {{n}}
= Writes a {n}-pixel-wide copy of every {ext} image in {folder}.
& folder=.

? size of the image {file} | dimensions of {file} | resolution of {file} | how big is the picture {file}
Get-ImageInfo -Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}"
% file=images
= Shows the width and height of {file}.
& folder=.

? image sizes in {folder} | dimensions of all {ext} in {folder}
Get-ChildItem -Path "{{folder}}\*" -File -Include {{ext}} | Get-ImageInfo
= Shows width and height of every image in {folder}.
& folder=. ; ext=*.jpg, *.jpeg, *.png, *.gif, *.bmp

? open {file} in paint | edit {file} in paint
Start-Process mspaint -ArgumentList "`"$((Resolve-Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}").Path)`""
= Opens {file} in Paint.
& folder=.

## Video and audio (ffmpeg)

? convert {file} to {ext} | convert the video {file} to {ext} | convert {file} into {ext}
$in = Get-Item -LiteralPath "{{folder}}\{{file}}"; $out = Join-Path $in.DirectoryName ($in.BaseName + '.{{ext|raw}}'); ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -stats -y -i $in.FullName $out; Get-Item $out
% file=videos,music ; ext=videos,music | Select-Object FullName, @{n='SizeMB';e={[math]::Round($_.Length/1MB,1)}}
= Converts {file} to {ext} with ffmpeg; the original stays.
& folder=.

? extract the audio from {file} | get mp3 from {file} | save the sound of {file} | convert the video {file} to mp3
$in = Get-Item -LiteralPath "{{folder}}\{{file}}"; $out = Join-Path $in.DirectoryName ($in.BaseName + '.mp3'); ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -stats -y -i $in.FullName -vn -q:a 2 $out; Get-Item $out | Select-Object FullName, @{n='SizeMB';e={[math]::Round($_.Length/1MB,1)}}
% file=videos
= Saves the soundtrack of {file} as an MP3.
& folder=.

? compress {file} | make the video {file} smaller | shrink the video {file} | reduce the size of {file}
$in = Get-Item -LiteralPath "{{folder}}\{{file}}"; $out = Join-Path $in.DirectoryName ($in.BaseName + '-small.mp4'); ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -stats -y -i $in.FullName -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset fast -c:a aac -b:a 128k $out; Get-Item $in, $out | Select-Object Name, @{n='SizeMB';e={[math]::Round($_.Length/1MB,1)}}
% file=videos
= Re-encodes {file} into a smaller MP4 (name-small.mp4); the original stays.
& folder=.

? cut {file} from {time} | trim the video {file} | first {seconds} of {file}
$in = Get-Item -LiteralPath "{{folder}}\{{file}}"; $out = Join-Path $in.DirectoryName ($in.BaseName + '-clip' + $in.Extension); ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -y -ss 0 -t {{seconds}} -i $in.FullName -c copy $out; Get-Item $out | Select-Object FullName
% file=videos,music
= Saves the first {seconds} seconds of {file} as name-clip.
& folder=. ; seconds=30

? length of the video {file} | how long is {file} | duration of {file} | video info {file}
ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration,size:stream=codec_name,width,height -of default=noprint_wrappers=1 "{{folder}}\{{file}}"
% file=videos,music
= Shows length (seconds), size, codecs and resolution of {file}.
& folder=.

? total length of {ext} in {folder} | how long are all videos in {folder}
$s = 0; Get-ChildItem -Path "{{folder}}\*" -File -Include {{ext}} | ForEach-Object { $s += [double](ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of csv=p=0 $_.FullName) }; [timespan]::FromSeconds($s).ToString('hh\:mm\:ss')
= Adds up the length of all {ext} files in {folder}.
& folder=.

? make a gif from {file} | convert {file} to gif
$in = Get-Item -LiteralPath "{{folder}}\{{file}}"; $out = Join-Path $in.DirectoryName ($in.BaseName + '.gif'); ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -y -t 10 -i $in.FullName -vf "fps=12,scale=480:-1:flags=lanczos" $out; Get-Item $out | Select-Object FullName, @{n='SizeMB';e={[math]::Round($_.Length/1MB,1)}}
% file=videos
= Turns the first 10 seconds of {file} into a 480-pixel-wide GIF.
& folder=.

? play {file} | listen to {file} | watch {file}
Invoke-Item -Path "{{folder}}\{{file}}"
= Opens {file} in your default player.
& folder=.

? is ffmpeg installed | ffmpeg version
ffmpeg -hide_banner -version | Select-Object -First 1
= Shows the installed ffmpeg version.

? install ffmpeg
winget install --id Gyan.FFmpeg --exact --accept-source-agreements --accept-package-agreements --disable-interactivity
= Installs ffmpeg, which Sensei uses for video and audio.
