# Disks, space and cleanup.

? free disk space | how much space is left | disk usage | drive space | free space | how full is my disk | storage left | which drive has the most free space | space on all drives
Get-PSDrive -PSProvider FileSystem | Where-Object Used -ne $null | Select-Object Name, @{n='UsedGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Used/1GB,1)}}, @{n='FreeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Free/1GB,1)}}, @{n='Free%';e={if ($_.Used + $_.Free) { [math]::Round(100*$_.Free/($_.Used + $_.Free)) }}}
= Shows used and free space on every drive.

? free space on {drive} | how much space is left on {drive} | space on {drive} | size of drive {drive}
Get-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} | Select-Object DriveLetter, FileSystemLabel, @{n='SizeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Size/1GB,1)}}, @{n='FreeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.SizeRemaining/1GB,1)}}
= Shows size and free space of drive {drive}.

? list volumes | drive letters | partitions | list drives
Get-Volume | Where-Object DriveLetter | Select-Object DriveLetter, FileSystemLabel, FileSystem, DriveType, HealthStatus, @{n='SizeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Size/1GB,1)}}, @{n='FreeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.SizeRemaining/1GB,1)}}
= Lists drive letters with label, file system, type, health and space.

? disk health | is my ssd healthy | physical disks | which disks do I have | is my disk an ssd
Get-PhysicalDisk | Select-Object FriendlyName, MediaType, BusType, HealthStatus, OperationalStatus, @{n='SizeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Size/1GB)}}
= Lists your physical disks with type, connection and health.

? ssd wear | disk temperature | disk errors | smart data | ssd health details
Get-PhysicalDisk | Get-StorageReliabilityCounter | Select-Object DeviceId, Temperature, Wear, ReadErrorsTotal, WriteErrorsTotal, PowerOnHours
= Shows temperature, wear %, errors and power-on hours.
! admin

? check {drive} for errors | scan the disk {drive} | chkdsk {drive} | check disk {drive}
Repair-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} -Scan
= Scans drive {drive} for file system errors without taking it offline.
& drive=C
! admin

? optimize {drive} | trim {drive} | defrag {drive} | optimize my ssd
Optimize-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} -Verbose
= Runs TRIM on SSDs or defragments hard disks for drive {drive}.
& drive=C
! admin

? usb drives | list usb disks | which usb sticks are connected
Get-Disk | Where-Object BusType -eq 'USB' | Select-Object Number, FriendlyName, @{n='SizeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Size/1GB,1)}}, HealthStatus
= Lists connected USB storage devices.

? eject {drive} | eject the usb {drive} | safely remove {drive} | unplug {drive} | eject usb drive {drive}
Dismount-Drive -Letter {{drive}}
= Safely ejects drive {drive} so you can unplug it (Sensei helper).

? eject the usb drive | eject usb | safely remove usb
Get-Volume | Where-Object DriveType -eq 'Removable' | Where-Object DriveLetter | ForEach-Object { Dismount-Drive -Letter $_.DriveLetter }
= Safely ejects every removable USB drive.

? format {drive} | format the usb {drive} | erase the drive {drive}
Format-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} -FileSystem exFAT -Confirm:$false
= ERASES EVERYTHING on drive {drive} and formats it as exFAT. Double-check the letter!
! admin

? rename drive {drive} to {name} | label drive {drive} {name}
Set-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} -NewFileSystemLabel "{{name}}"; Get-Volume -DriveLetter {{drive}} | Select-Object DriveLetter, FileSystemLabel
= Changes the label of drive {drive} to {name}.
! admin

## Cleanup

? size of the temp folder | how big are temporary files | temp size
Get-FolderSize -Path $env:TEMP
= Shows how much space your temp folder uses.

? clean temp files | delete temporary files | clear temp folder | free up space | clean up my pc | delete junk files
Clear-TempFiles
= Deletes what can be deleted in your temp folder and tells you how much space was freed (Sensei helper).

? recycle bin size | how big is the recycle bin
$n = @((New-Object -ComObject Shell.Application).Namespace(10).Items()).Count; $mb = (Get-ChildItem 'C:\$Recycle.Bin' -File -Recurse -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Measure-Object Length -Sum).Sum / 1MB; "{0} items, about {1:N0} MB on C:" -f $n, $mb
= Shows how many items are in the Recycle Bin and roughly how much space they take.

? disk cleanup | open disk cleanup | clean system files
cleanmgr.exe /d C
= Opens Windows Disk Cleanup for drive C:.

? storage sense | storage settings | what is using space on my disk | storage overview
Start-Process "ms-settings:storagesense"
= Opens Storage settings with a breakdown of what uses space.

? largest folders on {drive} | what uses space on {drive} | biggest folders on drive {drive} | what is taking up space on {drive} | what fills my {drive} drive
Get-FolderSize -Path "{{drive}}:\" -Subfolders
= Shows how much space each top-level folder on {drive}: takes (can take a few minutes).
& drive=C

? largest files on {drive} | biggest files on drive {drive}
Get-ChildItem -Path "{{drive}}:\" -File -Recurse -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Sort-Object Length -Descending | Select-Object -First 20 FullName, @{n='SizeGB';e={[math]::Round($_.Length/1GB,2)}}
= Lists the 20 biggest files on {drive}: (can take a few minutes).
& drive=C

? windows.old size | old windows installation | can I delete windows.old
if (Test-Path C:\Windows.old) { Get-FolderSize C:\Windows.old } else { 'There is no Windows.old folder.' }
= Shows the size of C:\Windows.old (remove it with Disk Cleanup > Clean up system files).

? turn off hibernation | delete hiberfil | disable hibernate
powercfg /hibernate off; 'Hibernation is off; hiberfil.sys was removed.'
= Turns hibernation off and frees the space of hiberfil.sys.
! admin

? turn on hibernation | enable hibernate
powercfg /hibernate on; 'Hibernation is on.'
= Turns hibernation on again.
! admin

? size of the downloads folder | how big is my downloads | downloads size
Get-FolderSize -Path "$HOME\Downloads"
= Adds up everything in Downloads.

? what uses space in my user folder | biggest folders in my profile | folder sizes in my home folder
Get-FolderSize -Path $HOME -Subfolders
= Shows how much space each folder in your user folder takes.
